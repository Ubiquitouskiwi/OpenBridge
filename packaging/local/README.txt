OpenBridge (use it on your own computer)
=======================================

This is OpenBridge in a single file. You do not need to install anything,
and you do not need the internet.

HOW TO START

  1. Unzip this folder.
  2. Double-click OpenBridge.html.
  3. It opens in your web browser. That's it.

  If it opens in the wrong program, right-click OpenBridge.html and choose
  "Open with", then pick your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).

YOUR PRIVACY

  - Everything you type stays on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  - When you close the browser tab, your answers are gone.
  - To keep your answers, use "Save my progress to a file". You choose a passphrase,
    and the saved file is locked with it.
  - On a shared or public computer, use "Clear my data / Start over" when you finish.

PLEASE NOTE

  OpenBridge is an early test version. The rules are drafts that experts
  have not checked yet. Results are estimates, not decisions. Only the agency
  that runs a program can decide if you qualify. If a result says you may
  not qualify, you can still apply.

  For free local help, call 211 or visit 211.org.
