# Welcome to the OpenBridge wiki

OpenBridge helps people find the public benefits they already qualify for. You answer one short set
of questions, and it checks SNAP, Medicaid and CHIP, WIC, and more at the same time. It explains
every result in plain words and helps you start the paperwork. Everything happens on your own
device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

![The OpenBridge results page, with an answer and reasons for each program]({{repo}}/raw/HEAD/docs/screenshots/results-top.png)

> **OpenBridge is a prototype.** The rules are drafts that experts have not checked yet. Results
> are estimates, not decisions. See [[Roadmap and status|Roadmap-and-Status]].

## Three things that make OpenBridge different

1. **It shows its work.** Every result can expand into the exact math, and each rule value links
   to the official source it came from.
2. **It is careful.** It will not tell you "no" when you are close to a limit, when an answer is
   missing, or because of immigration status alone.
3. **It keeps your information on your device.** No account, no tracking, nothing sent to a server.

## New here and want to help?

You do not need to be a programmer. The most valuable help right now is from people who know how
benefits work: caseworkers, navigators, legal aid staff, and policy researchers. Start with
[[Ways to help|Contribute-Without-Code]]. If you can spare one hour, the best first task is to
[[record a test case|Record-a-Test-Case]] for a household you see often.

New words? Check the [[Glossary]]. Still stuck? See the [[FAQ]] or email
openbridge@starlingtek.com.
