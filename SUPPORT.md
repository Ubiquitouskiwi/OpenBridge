# Getting help

## Need help applying for benefits?

OpenBridge cannot answer questions about your own case or tell you whether you qualify. For help
near you, call or text **211**, or visit [211.org](https://www.211.org/). Legal aid groups in your
state can help with applications, denials, and appeals.

## Questions about using OpenBridge

- Start with the [wiki](https://github.com/UbiquitousKiwi/OpenBridge/wiki), especially
  [Using OpenBridge](https://github.com/UbiquitousKiwi/OpenBridge/wiki/Using-OpenBridge) and the
  [FAQ](https://github.com/UbiquitousKiwi/OpenBridge/wiki/FAQ).
- Navigators and organizations: see the
  [Guide for navigators](https://github.com/UbiquitousKiwi/OpenBridge/wiki/Navigator-Guide) and
  [Host it for others](https://github.com/UbiquitousKiwi/OpenBridge/wiki/Host-OpenBridge).
- Still stuck? Email [openbridge@starlingtek.com](mailto:openbridge@starlingtek.com). Do not send
  real personal information.

## Found a problem?

Open an issue and pick the form that fits: bug, wrong rule or result, accessibility barrier,
wording or translation, feature idea, or new state. Use made-up details only.

Security or privacy problems are different: report them privately as described in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), never in a public issue.

## Response times

OpenBridge is maintained by a small volunteer team. We try to reply to issues within a week and to
security reports within 5 business days. There is no paid support.
